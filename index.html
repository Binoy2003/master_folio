Background & Narrative

About Me

I am a Computer Science and Engineering undergraduate at United International University (UIU) in Dhaka, Bangladesh, exploring data, technology, research, and storytelling.

My academic journey is shaped by a dual fascination: the quantitative rigor required to extract meaning from complex datasets, and the editorial discipline necessary to communicate those insights clearly to people.

Rather than treating programming solely as an implementation tool, I approach computing as a medium for structured inquiry. I am especially drawn to Data Science, data analysis, and machine learning principles—fields where formulating the right question is just as critical as computing the answer.

Parallel to my technical studies, I practice feature writing and storytelling. This allows me to bridge technical concepts with human narratives, exploring how modern software and computational systems interact with daily life and society. As an undergraduate student, I am focused on continuous learning, building principled foundations in algorithms and software engineering, and working toward meaningful research in data science.
